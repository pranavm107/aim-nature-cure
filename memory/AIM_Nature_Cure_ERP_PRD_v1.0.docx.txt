1. Executive Summary
AIM Nature Cure ERP is a purpose-built internal management system for AIM Nature Cure, a nature cure and wellness hospital. The system is designed to centralize all day-to-day clinical, operational, and financial activities into a single platform that the clinical and administrative team can use from any device.

Why it is being developed
Currently, patient records, therapy tracking, billing, follow-ups, and doctor performance are managed through informal or disconnected methods. This results in missed follow-ups, inconsistent revenue tracking, and limited management visibility. A structured ERP will formalize these workflows and make the hospital's operations transparent and auditable.

What problem it solves
•  Eliminates informal patient record-keeping
•  Enables systematic follow-up management to improve patient retention
•  Provides accurate revenue and incentive tracking for doctors
•  Gives management real-time visibility into hospital performance
•  Creates a structured workspace for doctors to manage consultations, therapy sessions, tasks, and social media activity

Primary Users
•  Admin — oversees all hospital operations, doctors, revenue, and reporting
•  Doctor — manages patient consultations, therapy sessions, follow-ups, tasks, and daily activity

What Version 1 will deliver
The first version of AIM Nature Cure ERP will deliver a fully functional system covering patient management, consultation and treatment records, therapy and package management, billing and payment tracking, doctor revenue calculation, configurable incentive rules, follow-up scheduling and reminders, lead source tracking, social media proof submission, doctor personal workspace, and daily, weekly, and monthly reporting.

2. Business Context
AIM Nature Cure is a nature cure and wellness hospital that offers treatments based on natural therapies. The hospital provides a range of services including steam baths, hip baths, and other established nature cure therapies, combined with consultations, diet guidance, and lifestyle recommendations.

2.1 Current Operational Workflow
Doctors
The hospital hires doctors who are responsible for patient intake, consultations, treatment planning, and therapy sessions. Since some doctors are hired from outside the local area and provided with on-site accommodation, they are available during extended hours and are expected to remain productive during patient-free intervals — including uploading social media content.

Therapy-Based Revenue
Revenue is generated through consultations and therapy sessions. Therapy packages are offered where patients can purchase a combination of sessions at a defined price. Doctors are expected to generate revenue through the therapies and packages they recommend and deliver.

Doctor Monthly Targets & Incentives
Each doctor is assigned a monthly revenue target. If the doctor's eligible revenue meets or exceeds the target, they receive a percentage-based incentive. Currently this tracking is informal; the ERP will automate it.

Patient Follow-Ups
After a patient's first visit, the doctor is expected to follow up with them a defined number of times (approximately three sessions based on initial requirements, but this will be configurable). Follow-ups are conducted by phone or WhatsApp. Currently there is no system to track whether follow-ups are completed or overdue.

Lead Generation & Social Media
Patients arrive through multiple channels including Google, Instagram, the hospital website, WhatsApp, referrals, and walk-ins. Tracking which source produces visits and revenue is a management requirement. Doctors are expected to post on social media regularly and submit proof of activity within the ERP.

Daily Accounting
The admin requires a daily view of payments received — including cash, UPI, card, and bank transfer — and whether the amounts have been correctly recorded. This is a financial control requirement.

Management Reporting
The hospital owner requires visibility into daily, weekly, and monthly performance metrics covering revenue, patient volumes, doctor performance, therapy performance, follow-up completion, and lead source effectiveness.

5. Functional Requirements
5.1 Authentication & Access Control
[FR-001]  The system shall provide a secure login screen with username/email and password.
[FR-002]  The system shall implement role-based access control (RBAC). Admins and Doctors see different dashboards and module sets.
[FR-003]  Users shall be able to reset their password via a secure flow.
[FR-004]  Admin shall be able to create, activate, and deactivate user accounts.
[FR-005]  Sessions shall expire after a configurable inactivity period.
[FR-006]  All authenticated actions shall be associated with the logged-in user for audit purposes.
[FR-007]  Access to all protected pages shall require a valid session. Unauthenticated access shall redirect to the login screen.

5.2 Admin Dashboard
The Admin Dashboard provides a real-time operational overview of the hospital.

[FR-008]  Display today's patient count, consultation count, and therapy session count.
[FR-009]  Display today's total revenue collected (all payment modes).
[FR-010]  Display count of active doctors.
[FR-011]  Display count of pending and overdue follow-ups.
[FR-012]  Display count of new leads today.
[FR-013]  Display monthly revenue progress against the period total.
[FR-014]  Display a doctor-wise revenue comparison for the current month.
[FR-015]  Display a chart showing patient volume over the past 30 days.
[FR-016]  Display a chart showing daily revenue over the past 30 days.
[FR-017]  Display a summary of follow-up completion rate.

5.3 Doctor Dashboard
The Doctor Dashboard is the primary daily workspace for each doctor.

[FR-018]  Display the doctor's patients scheduled for today.
[FR-019]  Display today's consultation count.
[FR-020]  Display today's therapy sessions.
[FR-021]  Display follow-ups due today and count of overdue follow-ups.
[FR-022]  Display the doctor's revenue generated today.
[FR-023]  Display the doctor's current month revenue, monthly target, and percentage achieved.
[FR-024]  Display the doctor's projected incentive for the current period.
[FR-025]  Display pending tasks and upcoming reminders.
[FR-026]  Display a prompt or status for today's social media activity submission.

5.4 Patient Management
[FR-027]  Admin and Doctor shall be able to register a new patient with the following details: full name, date of birth or age, gender, phone number, address, source of referral, and assigned doctor.
[FR-028]  Each patient shall be assigned a unique system-generated Patient ID.
[FR-029]  The system shall provide patient search by name, phone number, and Patient ID.
[FR-030]  Doctor shall be able to edit patient demographics and contact information.
[FR-031]  Patient status shall be tracked: Active, Inactive, Completed Treatment.
[FR-032]  Each patient record shall maintain a timeline showing all visits, consultations, therapies, payments, and follow-ups in chronological order.
[FR-033]  Admin shall be able to view all patients. Doctor shall be able to view their assigned patients.
[FR-034]  The system shall capture the referral or lead source at the time of patient registration.

5.5 Consultation & Medical Records
[FR-035]  Doctor shall be able to create a consultation record for any of their assigned patients.
[FR-036]  Consultation record shall include: date, chief complaint, medical history summary, clinical observations, diagnosis, treatment plan, diet and lifestyle recommendations, therapy recommendations, and doctor notes.
[FR-037]  Prescription field shall be available but is not mandatory.
[FR-038]  Consultation records shall be immutable once submitted. If corrections are needed, a follow-up note can be added.
[FR-039]  All past consultations for a patient shall be viewable in the consultation history.
[FR-040]  Admin shall be able to view all consultation records. Doctor shall only view consultations they created.
Note: Highly specialized clinical fields such as specific diagnostic codes or structured vitals are not required in Version 1 and should not be added unless explicitly requested.

5.6 Therapy Management
[FR-041]  Admin shall be able to create a therapy with the following fields: therapy name, description, duration (in minutes), standard price, and active/inactive status.
[FR-042]  Admin shall be able to edit and deactivate therapies.
[FR-043]  Inactive therapies shall not appear in new package or session creation.
[FR-044]  Doctor shall be able to assign one or more therapies to a patient as part of a treatment plan.
[FR-045]  Each therapy session assigned to a patient shall be tracked individually. Doctor shall mark each session as complete and record the date and any notes.

Note: Examples from requirements discussion include steam bath and hip bath. These are examples only. Admin can create any therapy appropriate for nature cure practice.

5.7 Therapy Packages
[FR-046]  Admin shall be able to create a therapy package with a name, description, total package price, validity period, and active/inactive status.
[FR-047]  A package shall consist of one or more therapies, each with a specified number of sessions.
[FR-048]  Example package structure: Steam Bath x 3 sessions + Hip Bath x 2 sessions = Total Package Price.
[FR-049]  Admin or Doctor shall be able to assign a package to a patient.
[FR-050]  When a patient is assigned a package, the system shall create the appropriate number of individual therapy sessions for tracking.
[FR-051]  The system shall track consumed sessions and remaining sessions for each active package.
[FR-052]  Package prices shall be fully configurable by Admin. No prices shall be hard-coded in the system.

5.8 Billing & Payments
[FR-053]  The system shall generate an invoice for each patient visit or service, including consultation charges, therapy session charges, and package charges.
[FR-054]  Invoice shall include: patient name, Patient ID, date, itemized services, quantities, individual prices, and total amount.
[FR-055]  Admin or Doctor shall be able to record payments against invoices.
[FR-056]  Supported payment modes: Cash, UPI, Card, Bank Transfer.
[FR-057]  The system shall support partial payments. Outstanding balance shall be tracked and displayed.
[FR-058]  Payment status on each invoice shall show as Paid, Partial, or Pending.
[FR-059]  Each payment transaction shall be recorded with the date, amount, mode, and recorded-by user.
[FR-060]  A printable or downloadable receipt shall be available for each payment.
[FR-061]  Refund handling is out of scope for Version 1. It will be considered as a future enhancement.

5.9 Doctor Revenue Tracking
This is a key business requirement. The system must calculate and display the revenue attributed to each doctor.

[FR-062]  Revenue attributed to a doctor shall be calculated from: consultations conducted by the doctor, therapy sessions delivered by the doctor, and packages assigned by the doctor.
[FR-063]  Revenue shall be viewable by today, current week, and current month.
[FR-064]  Admin shall be able to view revenue by individual doctor.
[FR-065]  Doctor shall be able to view their own revenue breakdown by patient and by service.
[FR-066]  Admin shall be able to view a comparative doctor-wise revenue report.
[FR-067]  The revenue calculation logic shall be based on paid amounts, not invoiced amounts, to avoid counting unpaid balances as revenue.
Note: Revenue attribution rules (e.g. whether a package assigned by Doctor A but sessions conducted by Doctor B counts toward which doctor) shall be clarified during implementation and documented as a business rule.

5.10 Incentive Calculation
Incentive calculation is a core custom feature of this ERP.

[FR-068]  Admin shall be able to configure an incentive rule per doctor with the following fields: target amount, target period (monthly), incentive percentage, and effective date.
[FR-069]  When a doctor's eligible revenue for the period reaches or exceeds the target, the system shall automatically calculate the incentive amount.
[FR-070]  Incentive amounts shall be displayed to the Admin for review and approval before payout.
[FR-071]  The Doctor shall be able to view their projected incentive based on revenue to date.
[FR-072]  No incentive values (targets or percentages) shall be hard-coded. All values are configurable by Admin.

Example Configuration (for illustration only)
Monthly Target: ₹30,000    |    Incentive Percentage: 5%
If doctor achieves ₹32,000 in eligible revenue, incentive = ₹32,000 × 5% = ₹1,600

Future Enhancement: Tiered Incentive Rules
The data model shall be designed to support tiered incentive rules in a future version, for example:
•  ₹0 – ₹20,000:  0% incentive
•  ₹20,001 – ₹30,000:  3% incentive
•  ₹30,001 – ₹50,000:  5% incentive
Note: Tiered incentives are not required in Version 1 but the schema should not prevent their addition.

5.11 Follow-Up Management
Follow-up management is a core operational requirement. The system must ensure that no patient follow-up is missed.

[FR-073]  Doctor shall be able to create a follow-up schedule for any patient, specifying the expected follow-up date and communication mode (Phone, WhatsApp, In-Person).
[FR-074]  The required number of follow-up sessions per patient shall be configurable by Admin. The default from requirements is approximately three sessions, but this must not be hard-coded.
[FR-075]  The system shall show today's scheduled follow-ups prominently on the Doctor Dashboard.
[FR-076]  The system shall automatically flag overdue follow-ups (follow-ups where the scheduled date has passed and no outcome was recorded).
[FR-077]  Doctor shall be able to record the outcome of a follow-up: Notes, Patient Response, and whether a next follow-up is required.
[FR-078]  After completing a follow-up, the Doctor shall be able to schedule the next follow-up from the same screen.
[FR-079]  Admin shall be able to view follow-up status for all patients across all doctors.
[FR-080]  The system shall display counts of: follow-ups completed today, pending follow-ups, and overdue follow-ups.

5.12 Reminders
[FR-081]  The system shall generate reminders for follow-ups scheduled for today and the next day.
[FR-082]  The system shall generate reminders for tasks with due dates approaching.
[FR-083]  The system shall surface overdue follow-up reminders on the Doctor Dashboard.
[FR-084]  Reminders shall be displayed as in-app notifications. External SMS or email integration is a future enhancement.

5.13 WhatsApp / Patient Communication
For Version 1, a lightweight WhatsApp communication workflow is sufficient and practical.

[FR-085]  Each patient record shall display the patient's phone number with a WhatsApp button.
[FR-086]  Clicking the WhatsApp button shall open WhatsApp (web or app) with the patient's number pre-filled. An optional predefined message template may be used.
[FR-087]  The Doctor communicates with the patient manually through WhatsApp.
[FR-088]  After communication, the Doctor shall record the outcome (notes, response, and next steps) in the follow-up record within the ERP.
Note: Direct WhatsApp API integration (automated messaging, delivery receipts) is out of scope for Version 1 and will be considered as a future enhancement.

5.14 Lead Management
Lead management allows the hospital to track where patients come from and which sources convert to revenue.

[FR-089]  Patient lead source shall be captured at registration. Supported sources: Google, Instagram, Hospital Website, WhatsApp, Doctor Referral, Patient Referral, Walk-In, Other.
[FR-090]  Admin shall be able to add custom lead sources.
[FR-091]  Admin shall be able to view a lead source report showing patient counts and revenue attributed per source.
[FR-092]  The system shall allow tracking of a lead through the following stages: Lead Captured, Contacted, Appointment Booked, Visited, Treatment Started, Payment Received.

5.15 Social Media Activity Tracking
Doctors are expected to post regularly on social media during available intervals. The ERP provides a mechanism to submit and verify this activity.

[FR-093]  Doctor shall be able to submit a social media activity entry with: platform (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, etc.), post type (Reel, Post, Story, Video), post date, post URL (optional), description, and screenshot or image proof (upload).
[FR-094]  Admin shall be able to view all social media submissions and mark them as Reviewed or Flagged.
[FR-095]  Admin Dashboard shall show a summary count of social media submissions today and this week.
Note: The system does not automatically verify activity on Instagram or any social media platform. All submissions are manual. Automated social media API integration is out of scope for Version 1.

5.16 Doctor Personal Workspace
The personal workspace is a private area for the doctor's own use and is not part of the official patient record.

[FR-096]  Doctor shall have access to a personal notes area where they can write free-text entries.
[FR-097]  Notes can include treatment ideas, patient planning thoughts, content ideas, or any personal professional content.
[FR-098]  Personal notes shall be visible only to the Doctor who created them. Admin shall not have access to personal notes.
[FR-099]  Doctor shall be able to search, pin, and archive personal notes.
Note: Doctor personal notes are strictly separate from official patient medical records. This distinction is critical and must be maintained in both the UI and data layer.

5.17 Doctor Tasks
[FR-100]  Doctor shall be able to create a personal task with: title, description, due date, priority (High / Medium / Low), and status (Pending / In Progress / Completed).
[FR-101]  Tasks shall be displayed on the Doctor Dashboard sorted by due date.
[FR-102]  Overdue tasks shall be highlighted.
[FR-103]  Doctor shall be able to set a reminder on a task.
[FR-104]  Example task: 'Call Arun Kumar for follow-up by Tuesday.'

5.18 Daily Closing / Daily Report
At the end of each working day, the Doctor submits a structured daily report to the Admin.

[FR-105]  Doctor shall be able to submit a daily closing report containing: patients handled, consultations completed, therapy sessions delivered, revenue generated, follow-ups completed, follow-ups pending, social media activity (summary), and any pending work to carry forward.
[FR-106]  Admin shall be able to view all doctor daily reports.
[FR-107]  Admin shall be able to flag or add comments to a daily report.
[FR-108]  The system shall indicate if a Doctor has not submitted a daily report by a configurable time.

5.19 Reporting
Daily Report
[FR-109]  Daily report shall include: patient count, consultations, therapy sessions, revenue (total and by payment mode), payments received, follow-ups completed vs. pending, new leads, and doctor-wise performance summary.

Weekly Report
[FR-110]  Weekly report shall include: patient growth (new vs. returning), total revenue, therapy-wise performance, doctor-wise revenue, follow-up completion rate, and lead conversion summary.

Monthly Report
[FR-111]  Monthly report shall include: total revenue, target achievement per doctor, incentive eligibility summary, patient growth, returning patient rate, therapy and package performance, lead source effectiveness, follow-up conversion, and payment reconciliation summary.

[FR-112]  Reports shall be viewable on-screen and exportable to PDF or CSV.

13. Development Strategy
The development approach is to select an existing open-source hospital ERP or HMS repository as a UI and application foundation, rather than building from scratch. This accelerates development while allowing full customization for AIM Nature Cure's specific requirements.

Development Steps
1.  Select a suitable open-source hospital ERP / HMS repository with a compatible UI structure and technology stack.
2.  Clone and run the repository locally to understand the existing architecture and module structure.
3.  Identify and remove modules that are not relevant to AIM Nature Cure's workflow.
4.  Rebrand the UI to AIM Nature Cure including name, logo, and color scheme.
5.  Modify the UI/UX to match the screens and workflows defined in this document.
6.  Design the AIM-specific database schema for patients, therapies, packages, billing, incentives, and follow-ups.
7.  Create or replace backend APIs to match the new data model.
8.  Implement therapy and package management workflows.
9.  Implement the incentive calculation engine with configurable rules.
10.  Implement follow-up scheduling, tracking, and reminder logic.
11.  Implement lead source tracking and social media proof submission.
12.  Implement daily, weekly, and monthly reporting.
13.  Implement RBAC, audit logging, and data security.
14.  Test all modules against acceptance criteria.
15.  Deploy to the production environment.

Note: The selected repository should be treated as a starting point, not a finished product. Significant modification will be required to match AIM Nature Cure's workflows.

16. Acceptance Criteria
Module
Acceptance Criteria
Authentication
Admin and Doctor can log in with their credentials. Each role sees only their permitted screens. Logout terminates the session. Password reset works.
Patient Management
A patient can be registered, searched by name or phone, edited, and viewed with their complete visit timeline including consultations, therapies, payments, and follow-ups.
Consultation
Doctor can create a consultation record for a patient. All required fields save correctly. Past consultations appear in the patient timeline.
Therapy Management
Admin can create, edit, and deactivate therapies. Doctor can assign therapies to patients and mark sessions as complete. Remaining sessions are tracked accurately.
Therapy Packages
Admin can create a package with multiple therapies and session counts. Package can be assigned to a patient. Session consumption is tracked. Remaining sessions update correctly after each session.
Billing
An invoice is generated with correct line items and total. Payment is recorded against the invoice. Payment status updates correctly (Paid / Partial / Pending). A receipt can be viewed.
Revenue Tracking
Doctor's daily and monthly revenue figures reflect actual paid amounts. Admin can view revenue per doctor.
Incentive Calculation
Admin configures a target and percentage for a doctor. When the doctor's eligible revenue reaches the target, the system calculates the correct incentive amount. Doctor can view their projected incentive.
Follow-Up Management
Doctor can schedule a follow-up for a patient. The follow-up appears on today's list when due. Doctor can record the outcome and schedule the next follow-up. Overdue follow-ups are flagged automatically.
Lead Management
Patient source is captured at registration. Admin can view a report showing patient counts and revenue by source.
Social Media
Doctor can submit a social media activity entry with screenshot. Admin can view all submissions and mark them reviewed.
Daily Closing Report
Doctor can submit an end-of-day report. Admin can view all submitted reports.
Reports
Admin can view daily, weekly, and monthly reports with accurate data for revenue, patients, therapies, follow-ups, and doctor performance.
RBAC
Doctor cannot access Admin-only screens. Admin can access all screens. Unauthorized URL access returns an appropriate access-denied response.
Audit Logs
All create, update, and delete actions on critical records are logged with the user, timestamp, and a description of the change.

17. Business Rules
Rule ID
Business Rule
BR-01
Only authenticated users can access any part of the system.
BR-02
A Doctor can only view and manage patients assigned to them. Admin can view all patients.
BR-03
Doctor personal notes are private. Admin cannot access them under any circumstance.
BR-04
Incentive rules are configured per doctor by Admin. No doctor shares another doctor's incentive rule automatically.
BR-05
Incentive target amounts and percentages are configurable and must never be hard-coded in the application.
BR-06
The required number of follow-up sessions per patient is a configurable system setting, not a hard-coded value.
BR-07
Therapy prices and package prices are configurable by Admin. No prices are hard-coded.
BR-08
An invoice must exist before a payment can be recorded. Payments are always linked to an invoice.
BR-09
Revenue calculations for incentive purposes shall use paid amounts only, not invoiced amounts.
BR-10
All financial changes (payments, invoice edits) must be auditable. No untracked financial modifications are permitted.
BR-11
Social media activity proof is manually submitted by the Doctor. The system does not claim to verify third-party platform activity.
BR-12
Lead source must be captured at patient registration and cannot be changed retroactively without Admin authorization.
BR-13
Consultation records are immutable after submission. Corrections are recorded as addendum notes.
BR-14
A deactivated therapy cannot be assigned to new patients but existing assigned sessions remain valid.
BR-15
Partial payments are permitted. Outstanding balances are tracked and visible on both the invoice and the Admin payment dashboard.

19.5 Final Product Vision

By centralizing what is currently fragmented across notebooks, messaging apps, and informal processes, the ERP will enable the hospital to scale its clinical team, improve patient retention through disciplined follow-up management, track the return on every marketing channel, and maintain the financial discipline that a growing wellness practice requires.
